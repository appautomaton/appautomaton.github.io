# Audio studio identity

Keep the approved studio: Bricolage Grotesque display type, Instrument Sans
reading text, IBM Plex Mono controls, Electric/Ember/Ultraviolet editions, and
independent light/dark themes. Fonts and assets are local to this module.
The page should not inherit the homepage's gallery layout or palette.

The animated SVG is decorative. Native scrolling drives layered motion; the
motion control and system reduced-motion preference disable it. Keyboard focus
stabilizes consoles. Content remains available without scripts.

The recording is prerecorded, with `preload="none"`, no autoplay, and native
controls as a fallback. Preserve all 15 model variants, their weight URLs, the
transcript, and code examples. Do not play audio during automated review.
